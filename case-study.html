Case Study - Contigo Mobility

Helping an EV mobility company connect with warm buyers across the Gulf region.

Project Overview

Autodataflow supported Contigo Mobility in identifying and engaging companies across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and surrounding Gulf markets interested in electric vehicle (EV) mobility and fleet solutions.

The outreach focused heavily on high-potential logistics operators, fleet management companies, hospitality-related transportation providers, and mobility infrastructure developers looking to transition toward cleaner, sustainable transportation.

The Challenge

Contigo Mobility wanted to expand into the rapidly growing GCC EV market but faced common regional barriers: identifying who holds the actual decision-making power for fleet electrification, bypassing generic info@ procurement firewalls, and validating which companies had real, active budgets and plans for fleet transitions.

Traditional lead lists were yielding cold, unresponsive prospects, and general bulk outreach was damaging the brand's premium reputation. They needed precise, relationship-driven market access.

The Strategy

Autodataflow deployed a highly targeted, multi-channel B2B connection workflow:

1. Precision Mapping: Instead of generic titles, we mapped the exact heads of sustainability, asset management, procurement, and fleet operations across major regional logistics providers, waterfront resorts, and municipalities.

2. Interest Validation & Multi-Channel Engagement: We engaged these targets across personalized LinkedIn, email, and direct WhatsApp channels, avoiding robotic pitches. Instead, we validated their current pain points with fleet electrification, charging timelines, and infrastructure constraints.

3. Opportunity Sourcing: Once interest and timing were verified, we introduced Contigo Mobility's team to the buyer, facilitating direct, warm commercial and technical discussions.
