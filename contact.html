Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers about our model, operational territories, and introduction mechanics.

How does your approach differ from standard lead generation agencies?
Standard agencies blast massive cold lists, focusing on quantity. We focus strictly on Buyer-Seller Introductions: identifying companies already showing relevant interest, validating demand, and creating direct warm connections with decision-makers.
Which target regions do you operate in?
We specialize in connecting suppliers with buyers across the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) countries—including Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, and Kuwait—and the European continent.
Which industries do you cover?
Our primary focus is on sustainable and advanced infrastructure, electric vehicle (EV) mobility, sustainable marine mobility, hospitality and waterfront development, fleet and logistics, and emerging B2B technology.
What is your introduction mechanism?
Once we identify and qualify a buyer's immediate business interest, we set up a direct professional introduction (via email or scheduled meeting), providing your team with full context to seamlessly start commercial negotiations.
Do you offer success-based pricing options?
Yes, for verified technical suppliers in our key focus areas, we offer hybrid engagement models that include milestone or performance-based incentives linked directly to successful introductions or SQL outcomes.
